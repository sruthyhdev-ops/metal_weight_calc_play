# Privacy Policy for Metal Weight Calculator

**Last Updated: October 24, 2026**

ZMOHI Studios ("we," "our," or "us") develops and operates the **Metal Weight Calculator** mobile application ("App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency regarding how your information is handled.

## 1. Information Collection

Metal Weight Calculator & PDF is designed to operate primarily offline and does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

Based on the application's current implementation:

- We do not independently collect, store on our servers, or sell users' personal information.
- We do not use advertising SDKs or display third-party advertisements.
- We do not integrate third-party analytics or dedicated crash-reporting services.
- We do not track user activity for advertising or marketing purposes.
- We do not operate a backend server to collect users' calculations, projects, or company information.

The App uses Google Play Billing for optional PRO purchases, as explained below.

## 2. Locally Stored Information

The App may store information locally on your device to provide its functionality, including:

- Metal weight calculations and custom material settings.
- Company profiles, business contact information, and logos.
- Saved projects, estimates, and quotations.
- Currency, measurement units, and other preferences.
- Locally generated PDF reports.
- Free PDF export usage and related application settings.

This information is processed and stored locally on your device and is not automatically uploaded to servers operated by ZMOHI Studios.

You can manage or delete locally stored information through available application features or your device's app data management settings.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data may permanently remove locally stored information.

## 3. Google Play Billing and PRO Purchases

The App offers optional premium features through Google Play Billing.

Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

- We do not collect or store credit card numbers, bank account details, or payment credentials.
- Payment processing is handled directly by Google Play.
- The App uses Google Play Billing to check purchase status and determine eligibility for PRO features.
- Purchase-related information may be processed by Google Play to complete transactions, manage purchases, and restore entitlements.
- We do not operate a separate purchase-history database or transmit purchase records to our own backend.

For more information, please review:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/)

## 4. Advertising and Analytics

The App does not contain third-party advertisements.

We do not integrate Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or other dedicated advertising and user-behaviour analytics services in the audited release.

We do not use advertising identifiers to deliver personalized advertisements.

## 5. PDF Generation and Sharing

PDF reports are generated locally on your device.

When you choose to export or share a PDF, the App may use Android's native file-sharing functionality.

The selected application or service receiving the shared PDF may process that file according to its own privacy policy.

ZMOHI Studios does not automatically upload generated PDF reports to external servers.

## 6. Data Security

Information generated or saved through the App is primarily stored on your device.

We do not maintain a remote database containing your saved calculations, company profiles, or PDF reports.

The security of locally stored information also depends on your device's security settings and operating system protections.

Google Play processes purchases through its own payment infrastructure.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear application data, or uninstall the App.

You can remove applicable saved information using the App's available deletion features or Android's application storage controls.

We do not maintain a separate user-account database.

Google may retain purchase and transaction information according to its own policies and legal obligations.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application functionality, integrated services, or applicable requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
